A security control plane for identity decisions

Steerpast IAM sits between identity data and sensitive actions. It combines identity and entitlement context with risk and policy evaluation, produces explainable decisions, and creates a remediation path with an auditable trail.

How it works

  1. Observe identity and entitlement context.
  2. Assess privilege, sensitivity, and behavioral risk.
  3. Evaluate policies and access constraints.
  4. Decide with an explainable security decision.
  5. Act through review, revocation, or another safe remediation path.

Built for humans and AI agents

  • Agent-aware identity and entitlement context
  • Risk and policy evaluation
  • Explainable access decisions
  • Remediation workflows and audit evidence
  • Continuous access review for privileged identities

Use cases

Monitor autonomous AI agents, detect privilege escalation, continuously review identity risk, and investigate suspicious access with a complete decision trail.
